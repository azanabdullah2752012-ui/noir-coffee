Tactile Craftsmanship

What Lies Inside The Box

Every component was authored for physical longevity and everyday tactile delight.

01

The Obsidian Keepsake Box

Custom-engineered rigid boxboard wrapped in FSC-certified charcoal paper with a velvety soft-touch finish and debossed copper foil typography. Hidden magnetic closure ensures a crisp, satisfying seal. Designed to live permanently on your kitchen counter or be repurposed.

Rigid Boxboard · Magnetic Foil Seal
02

The 15g Hand-Forged Scoop

Turned from pure solid copper with a hand-hammered bowl and stamped with our NOIR mark. Calibrated to dispense exactly 15 grams per level measure—eliminating the need for scales during quiet, meditative morning routines.

Solid Copper · 15g Dosing Weight
03

Three Spun Copper Canisters

Seamless spun copper canisters fitted with airtight food-grade silicone gaskets and one-way aroma degassing valves. Shields coffee beans from light, air, and humidity. Infinitely refillable with our zero-plastic compostable pouches.

3 × 250g · Degassing Valve
04

Letterpress Tasting Dossier

Letterpress-printed on 350gsm wild cotton paper using a vintage Heidelberg platen press. Features harvest lot coordinates, calibrated extraction dials for four brewing methods, and cupping notes written by our head roaster.

Heidelberg Letterpress · 350gsm Cotton
