Philosophy & Craft

Coffee deserves more attention than people give it.

We started NOIR because coffee had split into two dishonest extremes: supermarket brands roasted six months ago, or specialty coffee so intimidating you need a chemistry degree to order it. NOIR exists in between.

01 · Stillness

Before the world gets loud.

Think about your average morning. The alarm rings. The notifications arrive. Slack messages, news alerts, calendar invitations. Before your feet touch the floor, the demands of other people have already entered your head.

Coffee is one of the few daily habits you do entirely for yourself. Grinding whole beans by hand. Listening to the quiet rumble of the kettle. Watching the dry grounds bloom and swell under hot water. Taking the first sip from a warm ceramic cup.

Those fifteen minutes are not an indulgence; they are an anchor. We build coffees designed to reward that moment of quiet attention.

02 · Precision

Why convection airflow matters.

Most commercial roasters use traditional metal drum roasters. When coffee beans tumble against blistering 300°C cast-iron walls, the delicate surface oils scotch instantly. That scorching produces the ash, tar, and harsh bitterness that people mistake for "strong coffee."

At NOIR, we roast exclusively using closed-loop convection airflow on a Loring S35 Kestrel. Instead of scorching conductive metal, clean, super-heated air gently lifts and floats the beans in a cyclone of thermal energy.

Every single bean develops evenly from center to surface. Natural fruit sugars caramelize cleanly without burning. Delicate florals, stone fruits, and chocolate notes are preserved in pure botanical clarity.

03 · Materiality

Spun copper & zero waste.

Oxygen is the enemy of freshly roasted coffee. Traditional plastic-lined bags with tin ties leak volatile aromas within seventy-two hours of opening.

Every NOIR debut coffee ships sealed in an airtight, spun copper and deep burgundy steel canister. Engineered with food-grade nitrogen flushing and a silicone vacuum seal, it shields the beans from ambient light, moisture, and oxidation.

Our canisters are built to be reused for decades. When you subscribe, monthly deliveries arrive in certified compostable plant-fiber pouches—ready to refill your copper canister with zero single-use plastic waste.

04 · Physical Spaces

Visit the Tasting Rooms.

Designed as quiet sanctuaries away from street traffic. Dedicated to single-origin flights, seasonal cuppings, and manual brew rituals.

The Port Roastery

Rotterdam Tasting Room

Housed in an 1890s maritime warehouse along the Maas River. Featuring cast concrete, reclaimed timber benches, and our central copper brew counter overlooking the Loring convection roaster.

Address: Westerkade 14, 3016 CL Rotterdam Hours: Wednesday–Sunday, 07:30 – 16:00 Walk-ins welcome · Private cupping flights by appointment
The Alley Sanctuary

Tokyo Roastery

Tucked into a quiet residential lane behind Aoyama-dori. Built with traditional charred cedar (shou sugi ban), minimal raked limestone, and a six-seat counter dedicated to manual siphon and pour-over service.

Address: 3-12-8 Minami-Aoyama, Minato-ku, Tokyo Hours: Daily, 08:00 – 17:00 Six-seat bar · Omakase roast flights daily
