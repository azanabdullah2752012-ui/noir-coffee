Roast 02 · Nocturnal Roast

After Hours

For Those Who Prefer Depth

$25 / 250g spun copper canister

A darker, more contemplative roast crafted for quiet evenings, late-night reading, and those who prefer depth over sharp acidity. Convection airflow coaxes out deep caramelized sugars and rich cocoa oils while keeping the cup velvety, round, and free from charred bitterness.

Tasting Notes Rich Cocoa · Molten Caramel · Bourbon Oak
Purchase Option:
Grind Format:
Fresh batch roasted weekly · Dispatched within 48h of degassing

Harvest & Terroir

Sumatran volcanic soils yield heavy body and earthy cedar notes, tempered by the rich chocolate syrup profile of Colombian Huila beans. A slow roast curve develops dense natural oils without surface burning.

Origins Sumatra Gayo Highlands & Colombia Huila
Altitude 1,600m – 1,920m above sea level
Processing Wet-Hulled (Giling Basah) & Washed
Roast Method Extended convection caramelization profile

Sensory Profile

Full, syrupy, and satisfying. Formulated for heavy extraction, moka pot richness, or a midnight pour over dark chocolate.
