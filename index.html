Quiet Mornings

Before the morning rush begins and before the notifications arrive, there is a ten-minute window that belongs entirely to you.

Grinding whole beans, watching the bloom swell under hot water, feeling the weight of the ceramic cup in your hands. We started NOIR to make that morning moment count.

Small-Batch Convection Roasting

Traditional drum roasters burn delicate surface oils against scorching metal. We roast in small batches using clean convection airflow, preserving the pure origin sweetness of every harvest.

Each roast is sealed in our signature airtight spun copper and matte burgundy canisters to stay fresh until the final cup.
