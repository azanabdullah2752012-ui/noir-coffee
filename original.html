The Original

The Everyday Favourite

$24 / 250g spun copper canister

Our signature house blend is built for the quiet thirty minutes before your day demands answers. Balanced, smooth, and deeply reassuring. We combine high-altitude beans from Yirgacheffe and Antigua, roasting slowly with clean convection heat to coax out natural sweetness without a trace of bitter smoke.

Tasting Notes Dark Chocolate · Toasted Hazelnut · Dried Fig
Purchase Option:
Grind Format:
Fresh batch roasted weekly · Dispatched within 48h of degassing

Harvest & Terroir

We source directly from two generational washing stations. The high-altitude Ethiopian lot provides floral sweetness and crisp fruit acidity, balanced by the volcanic chocolate density of the Guatemalan Antigua harvest.

Origins Yirgacheffe (Ethiopia) & Antigua (Guatemala)
Altitude 1,750m – 1,920m above sea level
Processing Fully Washed & Sun-Dried on Raised African Beds
Roast Method Single-batch convection air (Loring S35)

Sensory Profile

A rounded, approachable cup profile formulated specifically for everyday milk drinks or black espresso.
