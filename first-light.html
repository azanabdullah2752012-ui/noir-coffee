First Light

For Slower Mornings & Curious Palates

$26 / 250g spun copper canister

A brighter, lighter roast roasted specifically for manual drip and pour-over clarity. Delicate, tea-like elegance that rewards slower mornings and curious palates. Luminous notes of bergamot citrus, wild jasmine petals, and ripe white peach with vibrant, sweet acidity.

Tasting Notes Bergamot Citrus · Wild Jasmine · White Peach
Purchase Option:
Grind Format:
Fresh batch roasted weekly · Dispatched within 48h of degassing

Harvest & Terroir

High-elevation micro-lots from the Bensa district of Sidama, intercropped with native shade trees and slow-dried. Blended with Kenya Nyeri SL28 varietals for dazzling phosphoric brightness and stone-fruit sweetness.

Origins Sidama Bensa (Ethiopia) & Nyeri (Kenya)
Altitude 1,950m – 2,150m above sea level
Processing Double Fermented Washed & Extended Drying
Roast Method Gentle convection profile preserving delicate aromatics

Sensory Profile

Crisp, floral, and crystalline. Formulated for Chemex carafes, manual drippers, or refreshing cold-brew infusions.
